Leg asset and integration

upper and distal/claw links. Original PNG copied without pixel edits. Generator,
source file and SHA-256 are recorded in assets/blue_ink/centipede-leg-generation.json.
Godot AtlasTexture selects links; native mipmaps prevent broken small-scale lines.
Measured hip/knee/claw pivots align actual painted joints with a two-link IK rig.
The body hides hip attachment; left/right links are mirrored; adjacent legs have
staggered phases driven by actual segment travel. Stance feet stay in world space.
Swing and controlled replant handle turns; idle stops stepping. Head antennae,
player physics, collision, speed and gameplay data are unchanged.
