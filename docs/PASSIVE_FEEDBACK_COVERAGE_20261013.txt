WHAT “强化” MEANS
reward_choice formerly used generic “变异强化” and “选择后立即应用此项强化”.
Actual level-up titles are “{器官}升级”. Reward_WallCores was “强化装甲” in
chinese_ui but insect_armaments overrides it with “铁鞘”, giving ×2/3/4 铁鞘.
The user's exact card cannot be identified solely from the word 强化.
Count21 adds banked wall charges. It does NOT reinforce a segment until the user
selects a body module and chooses 加固. Reinforcement adds hull health, not a shield.
An upgrade for an absent equipment family is stored for future matching equipment;
it must not show an active shield or link to an unrelated body part.

ACQUISITION / PERSISTENCE MATRIX
All baseline rewards only enqueue a title notification in run_state.take_reward.
BlockReward: pending module; chain always grants one segment, regardless of old
coresCount variants. Show activation on installation, not on acquisition.
CountBasicReward: pending action charges. Type21 armor; 8 swap;9 duplicate;20 move.
PercentBasicReward: permanent corresponding stat (2 rate,3 range,10 head health,
11 movement,12 boost,13 head rate,14 head damage,15 body weapon damage,
16 body health,17 head range). Capped formulas can limit the actual resulting stat.
CrusedReward: apply BOTH positive and negative stat changes, not a universal buff.
MainCoreTurretReward: replace only head weapon; shield replacement needs actual
shield initialization/reset and shared effective-core lookup across all consumers.
MainCoreDroneReward: add 2/4 attack, repair, or scrap head drones; obey maximum.

Continuous flame has no discrete rate-of-fire mechanism; do not advertise that
field as functional. Head/body targeting must follow matching effective weapon ID.
ShieldLevelUpReward: matching shield +50% core life/capacity/recharge,
-50% recharge delay,+20% range per level; never create a shield without equipment.
FuelTankLevelUpReward: matching fuel tank +50% life/capacity per level.
MineFieldLevelUpReward: field +25% life/max mines, -25% reload interval;
spawner +25% life,-10% spawn interval. Spawner maxMinesCountPercent is unused.
DroneLevelUpReward: add one BAY drone per level, not an extra HQ drone; matching
existing/future drone IDs also gain speed/efficiency/range. Attack bay core life
field is 0.649, not rounded to an invented 0.65. Engineer/scrapper core life is zero.
LevelUpReward (unpooled wall): +25% hull life, no energy shield.

ORIGINAL MECHANIC FINDINGS (NOT JUST VISUAL)
HQ shield: combat step recharged hq_weapon, but hurt_player/shield_range/
player_visual_contact used head-body ID, causing no protection/missing ShieldRange;
switching away retained stale shield. Must use effective_core and reset on swap.
Continuous flame: damage call omitted family DamagePercent and HQ stat14;
actual cone range omitted RangePercent and global range despite target acquisition.
Field mine recharge: negative interval bonus incorrectly multiplied a frequency,
slowing reload. Spawner interval formula already had correct direction.
Health debuff: refresh_upgrades could leave HP over reduced max and could resurrect
dead parts on positive upgrade. Clamp live HP and preserve dead-state.

IMPLEMENTED FIXES AND REVIEW RESULT
Shared effective_core/set_hq_weapon lookup and replacement reset make head shields
protect correctly and remove obsolete shield state when changing weapons.
Live hull HP clamps to the new maximum; positive upgrades preserve dead-state.
Feedback uses stable part UIDs, a bounded event queue, exact reward statuses,
chain-specific installation hooks and persistent summed upgrade levels.
Stat cues target actual consumers: projectile body range, attack-only bay/drone
attack modifiers (including head drones), projectile-only head gun modifiers.
ContinuousProjectile fire-rate cues are excluded because that field is unused.
Continuous damage and cone range now include the applicable global/family bonuses.
Minefield reload uses base interval*(1+bonus), with .05sec minimum for field and
spawner. This interpretation is inferred from negative catalog values and the
existing spawner formula, not certified Unity/C# equivalence. Field level0/1/2
intervals are1/.75/.5sec; spawner intervals are5/4.5/4sec.
Legacy charge actions have correct names; unused spawner maximum/flame fire-rate
fields are excluded from summaries; bay drone count is distinguished from HQ count.
Six generated AtlasTexture overlays cover shield/armor/repair/chip/boost/cloak.
Active fuel tanks have five-pip fill/boost cues; mine deployments have emitter
pulses; carrying drones have cargo diamonds; upgraded drones have correct ranks.
The stat ledger remains visible and ignores mouse input. Original localized attack
and pack-drop visuals are retained. Healing cues mark the actual healed target.
Repair-drone tethers require an unstunned owner, their own healed target and actual
action-range proximity, avoiding shared-owner false positives across drones.
Shield-hit arcs use the real boundary; notices have bounded text and layout.
No outstanding blocking false-active or lifecycle defect was found in this review.
