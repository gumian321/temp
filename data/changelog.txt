CENTIPEDE / ARM EACH SEGMENT

Explore a living underground world as a centipede or spider. Equip attack organs,
support organs and protective chitin, then evolve your body through mutation rewards.

Six operations: survive, endure endless waves, hunt a brood, escort a migrating brood,
break an enemy nest, or defend your own nest. Mining ore releases energy crystals.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click to attack, Shift to boost,
E for body assembly, Space for your active ability, and Esc to pause.
Body modules can be moved, exchanged, copied and reinforced during assembly.
